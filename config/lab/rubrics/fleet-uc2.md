# UC2 experimental asset criteria

Experimental criteria for calibration, not owner-rated anchors or an approved aesthetic verdict.

Inspect renders and stats against the brief. Consider silhouette, geometry defects, material fidelity, UV/base colour and measured target constraints. Do not inspect the GLB. Ignore display order. A tied comparison means the evidence does not distinguish the candidates.

Scale anchors: 1 unusable or wrong object; 2 recognizable with major geometry or material defects; 3 usable silhouette with visible defects; 4 meets brief with minor defects; 5 meets brief with no material observed defect. Explain observed defects. Pass means all explicit brief constraints are met; fail identifies the violated constraint.

Owner-rated pass/fail examples are not yet available. Do not fabricate examples or infer unattended acceptance from these criteria. The evaluator prompt override must include these criteria and the pipeline output instructions; its content changes the recorded evaluator version.
